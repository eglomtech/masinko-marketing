ABOUT US

Named after an instrument that carries.

The masinqo is a single-stringed Ethiopian instrument. It has one string and no amplifier — yet its sound travels across a whole room. That is exactly what good content does for a brand.

We started in Addis Ababa in 2019 with one belief: Ethiopian businesses do not need louder marketing, they need clearer marketing. Content that sounds like the people it is speaking to — in Amharic, English, or both.

Today we are a bilingual team of strategists, creators and media buyers working with local brands and with international companies entering the Ethiopian market.

OUR MISSION

To help every Ethiopian brand we work with be understood — clearly, consistently, and in the language their customers actually speak.
